# Dem Cyaan - Ras Manuel
Its own project: everything here comes from this song's lyrics only (lyrics.txt).

v1 (images\v1) was rejected 2026-10-23: it showed the singer acting the lyrics out literally. Justice: art is an extended metaphor that makes you feel the specific details without showing the subject.

## v2 metaphor: El volcan
The fire in the lyrics as a volcano that slept for centuries looking like a quiet green mountain (tanto tiempo esperando, muchas veces callado), then woke (ahora todo es diferente). Storms (the envious ones) pour rain on the lava and only make steam; the ocean throws its waves at it and the lava builds new land (they can't stop the fire). The same heat feeds the hot springs that heal (este fuego sana).

## Art style
**Romantic sublime landscape oil painting.** Thick impasto and glowing glazes give the fire weight and scale, and the human-sized feeling of facing something unstoppable.

Style sentence (word for word in every prompt):

Romantic sublime landscape oil painting with thick impasto and glowing atmospheric glazes, dramatic scale and luminous light

Recurring element (word for word):

a perfect steep cone volcano rising out of dense green rainforest
