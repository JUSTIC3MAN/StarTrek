## Song
A woman remembering when this life seemed a dream and now living what she was meant to, despite being told not to. A flower in her garden reaching for the sun, roots holding tight, petals blown to the sun, never letting go; the sea breeze she rises with ("como caminas cuando te levantas con la brisa de este mar"); refusing to be the girl who stays up waiting; ends as the conqueress of a new time.
## Metaphor
One flower growing out of a crack in an old sea wall: told it couldn't, it does. Night -> sunrise = the dream becoming the life. Chorus: the sea breeze lifts its petals into a ribbon that rings the sun (heartbeat ring, "latido"). Light is the subject; built-in motion (petals, breeze, rays).
