# Cruzando Fronteras - Ras Manuel
Its own project: everything here comes from this song's lyrics only (lyrics.txt).

Rejected 2026-10-23: v1 (images\v1) showed the singer acting out the lyrics; v2 used a nature-documentary metaphor. Justice: music-video frames that are cool and aesthetic, carry the song's own identity, and use metaphor instead of literal depiction.

## v3: Lines and thresholds at night
Borders as lines, fences, roads and maps, crossed at night with dancehall energy: a step over the line, fence shadows on the sound system, the road ahead, a map tearing loose, the crowd's hands. A figure appears only as a fragment or silhouette.

Style sentence (word for word in every prompt):

gritty neon night photography for a dancehall music video, wet streets, sodium orange and electric green light, motion streaks, heavy grain and deep black shadows

# v4 (2026-10-23): research-picked art style, no photorealism
Justice rejected v1-v3: too realistic, and the styles weren't backed by research. v4 style chosen from download rankings of the 1,500+ Krea 2 style LoRAs on Hugging Face (ilkerzgi), checked against Creative Boom's 2026 top-illustrators survey.

**Orange-blue riso etching: #23 by downloads (263). Risograph is gig-poster and zine print culture, and two inks (sodium orange, night blue) suit a night-crossing dancehall song.**

LoRA: `orange-blue-riso-etching-comfy.safetensors` at 1.1, trigger `orange blue riso etching style` at the end of every prompt.
