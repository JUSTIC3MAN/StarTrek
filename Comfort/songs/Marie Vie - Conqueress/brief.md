## Song
A woman representing female power and liberation; "come carry your heart, I go now"; the sun that warms you, the fire from Mars; dawn; climbing the highest mountain, searching the meadow and the sea, raising the sails of the ship; lighting her flor de cana to enter the battle; the heartbeat of a lion.
## Metaphor
A small boat at night, its sail tied down: she unties it and the sail fills ("I go now", and the song's own "levantar las velas del navio"). The chorus is the sun itself, carved as rings and rays, a red spark of Mars above it, the rings turning to a ring of fire. The woman is only a small fully clothed silhouette.
