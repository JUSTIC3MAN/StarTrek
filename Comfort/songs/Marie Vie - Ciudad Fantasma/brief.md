# Ciudad Fantasma - Marie Vie
Its own project: everything here comes from this song's lyrics only (lyrics.txt).

Rejected 2026-10-23: v1 (images\v1) showed the singer acting out the lyrics; v2 used a nature-documentary metaphor. Justice: music-video frames that are cool and aesthetic, carry the song's own identity, and use metaphor instead of literal depiction.

## v3: A town that is empty but still lived in
The ghost town as absence that still moves: clothes on the line filled by the wind, a marimba the wind plays, a bell rope swinging alone, the jungle covering the mural, windows still lit in an empty street. No people.

Style sentence (word for word in every prompt):

dreamy faded 35mm film still for a music video, soft fog, muted desaturated color, gentle halation and fine grain
