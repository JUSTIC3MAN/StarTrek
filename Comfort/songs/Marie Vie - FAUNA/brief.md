## Song
An island where "multiple lives" live, "the brightest of the place to be"; finding the light so you return, the air that heals, a mountain hidden by the sea that wakes and understands the secret and the truth; nature revealing a sacred encounter; flying through clouds, moons, sunsets, a love waiting on the island.
## Metaphor
The island's lives as tiny lights (fireflies, glowing plankton) that rise from the lagoon at dawn toward a mountain hidden in mist; in the chorus the mist tears open, white seabirds spiral up and ring the peak of the mountain the sea was hiding - the light found, the return. The fauna is the song's own world (island), shown as light and rings, never a documentary.
